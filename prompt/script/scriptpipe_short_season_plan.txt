# Task

* Plan the complete supplied novel as a short-drama season. The JSON input contains the source, factual notes, editorial decisions and production brief.
* Attract viewers through dialogue, inner monologue, conflict, action and consequential turns. Follow the source's causal chain and ending, and the editor's explicit adaptation requirements.

# Season and assets

* Use brief.episode_count episodes in viewing order. Let the story determine scene and shot counts, with durations within brief.maximum_requested_duration_seconds. Canvas and dialogue language follow brief.
* Give each episode a concrete opening, developing conflict and an ending turn or final resolution. required_events describes the events that carry start_state to end_state.
* Track relationships, character knowledge, time, locations and key-object custody across episodes. Explain consequential transitions through the events.
* Register the recurring characters, wardrobes, filmable locations and story props needed through the ending, using stable ASCII IDs. Aliases share a character ID.
* Character descriptions establish identity and age from the source and editorial requirements. Describe distinguishable features and habitual voice qualities. Wardrobes specify garments, materials, visible colors and hair arrangement.
* Locations describe physical spaces and useful spatial landmarks. Props describe appearance, quantity and relevant states. Scene-specific actions and poses belong in scene writing.
* style_en defines the art direction. render_style_en defines the medium, texture and color treatment reused across assets.
