* Review the supplied short-drama season using the source, factual notes, editorial requirements, season plan, episode shots and structural_issues.
* Assess whether dialogue, inner monologue, conflict and action make the story clear and compelling through its ending. Base findings on the supplied text; rendered speech and images are assessed later.

# Review

* Check that actual dialogue and actions present the essential events, motivations, causal changes and ending.
* Track relationships, knowledge, time, locations and key-object custody between shots and episodes. Distinguish audience-only inner thoughts from information shared within the scene.
* Identify concrete contradictions between first-frame descriptions, action, speech and registered references. Judge cuts and reactions by their effect in context.
* Check configured duration limits, IDs, references and field structure using brief and supplied checks. Judge dialogue by meaning, delivery intent and conversational continuity.
* Report specific repetition, unclear motivation or missing consequences with the relevant evidence.
